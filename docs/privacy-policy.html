01

Overview

Nomnom is an app that helps you pick a restaurant nearby — either an instant pick, a five-way elimination bracket, or a curated list — and, optionally, lets you do that together with a friend in real time. This policy covers the Nomnom mobile app for Android, operated by Isaac Finger ("we", "us").

Nomnom does not require you to create an account with an email or password. Most of what it stores lives only on your own device.

02

Information we collect

CategoryWhat, specificallyRequired?
Precise location Your device's GPS coordinates, while the app is in use. Never collected in the background. Required to search

We do not collect photos, video, or audio you record. Every photo shown in the app is pulled from Google's own listing for that restaurant, not uploaded by you. We also do not collect the Android Advertising ID — see Section 4.

03

How we use it

  • Finding restaurants: your location is sent to Google Places to search nearby spots matching your filters.
  • Spinning with a friend: when you start a shared session, both players' locations are used to compute a fair midpoint and search radius between you.
  • Your food journal: ratings and notes you write are mirrored so mutual friends — people you've actually played a session with — can read them.
  • The Feed: a prototype photo feed pulls from restaurant photos your own device has already seen, plus a shared pool built from everyone's searches. This shared pool stores restaurant/photo data only — never anything that identifies who searched for it.
04

Shared with third parties

Two infrastructure providers process data on our behalf. Neither is an advertising or analytics network — we don't use either.

  • Google Places receives your location and search filters (cuisine, radius, price) each time you search, in order to return nearby restaurants. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud) hosts the anonymous authentication, the friend-session data, and the journal mirror described below.

Advertising

Nomnom includes no advertising or analytics SDK of any kind, and does not read or use the Android Advertising ID. No data is collected or shared for advertising purposes — this app's Google Play Data Safety declaration reflects that directly.

05

Shared with other users

Nomnom has no public profiles and no stranger-matching — the only people who can see anything of yours are people you've directly played a session with.

  • During a live "Feast with Friends" session, the other player sees your chosen display name and your location (used to compute the shared search area).
  • Once you've completed a session with someone, they're added to your friends list, and your food journal entries become visible to them going forward.
06

Where it's stored

Most of what Nomnom remembers — your search history, favorites, saved-for-later list, and app preferences — is stored locally on your device only, and is never transmitted anywhere.

Three things are stored remotely, in Firestore (Google Cloud), because they need to sync between devices or between you and a friend:

  • Active and past "Feast with Friends" sessions (both players' locations, turn state, chosen restaurants).
  • Your friends list mirror, so a friend request or ping can reach you on a different device.
  • Your food journal, mirrored so mutual friends can read it.
07

Retention & deletion

Locally stored data (history, favorites, preferences) stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Data mirrored to Firestore persists until you ask us to remove it.

There's currently no in-app "delete my account" button. To request deletion of any data associated with your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below and we'll remove it.

08

Children's privacy

Nomnom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. The app does not currently ask for a birthdate or otherwise verify age.

09

Your choices

  • Location: you can deny the location permission at any time in your device settings. Nomnom can't search for restaurants without it, but nothing else in the app requires it.
  • Friend features: entirely optional. "Pick 4 Me," "Eliminate," and "Curated" all work solo with nothing shared or stored remotely.
  • Deletion: see Section 7.
10

California privacy rights

If you're a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, gives you specific rights over the personal information described in Section 2:

  • Right to know what personal information we've collected about you.
  • Right to delete that information (see Section 7 for how).
  • Right to correct inaccurate information we hold about you.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not "share" it as CCPA defines that term (disclosure for cross-context behavioral advertising) — Nomnom has no advertising SDK to share it with in the first place.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 13. We may ask you to confirm your device's anonymous ID to verify the request before completing it.
